From first loan to last payment

What loan management looks like in Acervus

A notebook or a spreadsheet can hold the numbers, but every payment, late installment and capital movement means more arithmetic. Acervus follows each loan through its whole life and does that arithmetic the same way every time.

  1. Add the client

    Contact details and an ID photo, with every loan and payment kept on the client's record. Find anyone by name, phone or email.

  2. Set up the loan

    Installment or interest-only, collected daily through monthly. You see the full schedule before you confirm, and you can adjust it later.

  3. Record each payment

    Full or partial. Before you save, Acervus shows how the payment splits between interest, capital and late fees.

  4. Follow up on what is late

    Overdue and soon-due loans come to the top with the exact amount to collect. Late fees are added on their own, and you decide when to waive them.

FAQ

Questions about Acervus

What types of loans can I manage?

Installment loans, with interest on the declining balance or flat interest, and interest-only loans. Payments can fall due daily, weekly, every two weeks or monthly. For an installment loan you see the whole schedule before you confirm it, and you can adjust it later.

Can a client pay only part of an installment?

Yes. Record whatever amount the client paid. By default it goes to the oldest installment first: its late fee, then interest, then capital. On an installment loan you can instead put it on one installment, spread it over several, or set the split yourself, and Acervus shows the result before you save.

How are late fees calculated?

Each installment loan has its own late-fee percentage. If an installment still has capital or interest unpaid at the end of its due date, Acervus adds a late fee the next day: that percentage of the installment's unpaid capital plus its unpaid interest. Payments dated on or before the due date reduce that amount, and earlier late fees are not part of it. Each installment is charged a late fee once. You can waive a late fee, or set the percentage to zero for loans that carry none.

How do I see outstanding capital and what each loan still owes?

Every loan shows the amount needed to pay it off, how much of it has been paid back and its next payment. The overview adds up capital lent out, available capital, interest earned and overdue amounts across the business.

Can I keep many clients and loans in one place?

Yes. A client can hold several loans, and each loan keeps its own schedule, payments and late fees. Find a client by name, phone or email, and filter loans to see the open, overdue or closed ones.

Does Acervus work without internet?

The web and phone app has to be opened online on a device first. After that, if the connection drops, you can still record payments, new clients, expenses and income; they are sent when you reconnect. New loans and edits need a connection, and a payment recorded offline gets its receipt and counts in your totals once it has synced. The Windows app needs a connection to sign in; after that it remembers you and opens your business's copy on the PC even offline. There, too, new clients, payments, expenses and income can be saved without a connection: they appear in the PC's copy at once and are sent to the cloud, oldest first, when it reconnects. Other changes need a connection.

Can my collectors use it?

Yes. Invite them by email as collectors or viewers. Collectors record payments, clients, expenses and income, and their payments wait for an admin to confirm them. You choose which loan details collectors can see. Viewers can look but not change anything.

Does Acervus lend money?

No. Acervus is software to keep your lending business organized. It does not lend, hold or collect money for you.

Is my information private?

Acervus doesn't show ads and doesn't sell or rent your information, and each business is kept apart from the others. The Privacy Policy explains where data is stored and how to delete it.
